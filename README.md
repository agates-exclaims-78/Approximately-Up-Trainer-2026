## 🎮 About the Game

Approximately Up is a first-person space simulation adventure developed and published by Approximately Games. It released on Steam on August 6, 2026. Players build modular spacecraft, wire automated systems, pilot their creations from inside the ship, complete missions, collect components, and explore 15 planets alone or in online co-op for up to four players.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Approximately Up**. This trainer adds convenience options for building, piloting, exploration, and mission progression in Approximately Up. It can reduce the effects of crashes and environmental hazards, simplify ship operation, and provide practical controls for testing spacecraft designs without changing the game's core construction and flight systems.

**Target Audience:** Useful for players who want to experiment with modular spacecraft, test automated flight systems, explore planets more freely, recover from crashes, or reduce the time required to unlock components and complete missions.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Approximately Up uses first-person movement inside spacecraft and on planetary surfaces. The jetpack is useful for navigating difficult terrain and returning to a ship. Use the movement options only when needed, since unrestricted movement can make it easier to leave the intended play area or lose contact with the spacecraft.

### Tools & Inventory

Ship components are collected and used to expand or rebuild spacecraft. Build-related options are best used while testing designs in a garage or station. Avoid changing inventory or unlock states while a mission is active, as doing so may interfere with normal reward and progression handling.

### World & Environment

The game begins on Earth and gradually opens a system of 15 planets with different gravity, atmospheres, temperatures, sizes, and hazards. Planet Stations act as checkpoints, garages, and launch locations. Hazard protection and safe-flight options are especially useful when reaching new destinations or testing ships in unfamiliar environments.

### Combat & Weapons

Approximately Up does not have a conventional character-combat system. Its main challenges come from spacecraft construction, physics, navigation, environmental dangers, missions, and the risk of crashing. The trainer therefore focuses on survival, flight testing, ship protection, and exploration rather than weapons or combat statistics.
